What the app does

HabitGlass is a habit tracking app for iPhone. You create habits, log completions, and track your progress. All data is stored locally on your device. The app offers both a free tier and a paid subscription tier (HabitGlass Pro) with additional features.

Your account and data

There is no account. HabitGlass doesn't require registration, login, or an email address. Your data lives on your iPhone, and you're responsible for it.

If you enable iCloud sync (a Pro feature), your data is mirrored to your private iCloud storage through Apple's CloudKit. I don't have access to that data. Apple's iCloud terms apply to that sync.

If you delete the app, your local data is gone. If you use the in-app "Delete All Data" option, that's permanent too. I can't recover deleted data because I never had it in the first place.

Free and Pro tiers

The free tier includes:

  • Up to 3 active habits
  • 6 habit colors
  • Daily reminders
  • Full tracking and streak history

HabitGlass Pro unlocks:

  • Unlimited habits
  • Full color palette
  • Monthly insights
  • Data export (JSON and CSV) and import
  • Custom categories
  • Day notes on check-ins
  • Advanced reminders (nudge follow-ups, interval reminders)
  • Multiple completions per day
  • iCloud sync
