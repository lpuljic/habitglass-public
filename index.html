One tick per reminder.

Set a daily goal and a reminder schedule, and HabitGlass splits the goal into one share per reminder. Tap through a day below.

Water

2,000 mL a day, with a reminder every hour from 7:30 am to 7:30 pm. That's 13 reminders, so each tick is about 160 mL.

0/13 0 of 2,000 mL

The last tick only fills at 2,000 mL. Then the day is done and the reminders stop.

Reading

30 pages a day with 6 reminders, so each tick is 5 pages. Prefer the clock? Set a minutes goal and pages become optional.

0/6 0 of 30 pages

A reading reminder opens the log so you can say how much you read. Nothing gets checked off for you.

Fair questions.

Do I need an account?
No, and there's no way to make one. No login screen, no profile, no server storing your habits, which is also why there's nothing for me to leak.
Does it work offline?
Completely. Everything is computed on device. The only network call asks RevenueCat whether you have Pro, and the app copes fine when the answer never arrives.
Which iOS version do I need?
iOS 26 or later. The interface is built on Liquid Glass, mesh gradients and SwiftData, none of which exist on older releases.
Is there an Android or web version?
No, and there's no plan for one. HabitGlass leans hard on iOS APIs, and a port would be a different, worse app.
Can I get my data out again?
Yes. Pro includes a JSON export that a JSON import restores, habits and history included, plus CSV for spreadsheets.
Do I lose a streak on a rest day?
Not if the schedule says it's a rest day. Streaks are counted against each habit's own schedule, so a three-times-a-week habit doesn't punish you for the four days you were never meant to do it.
Can I switch from monthly to lifetime?
Yes. Buy lifetime in the app, then cancel the subscription from your Apple account settings so it doesn't renew.
Something's broken. Who do I tell?
Me, directly. No ticket portal, no chatbot: support@habitglass.info.
