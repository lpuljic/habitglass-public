How Health habits work ↗

  • Widgets

    Check off today's habits from the Home Screen, as a list or one at a time. Your streak sits on the Lock Screen.

  • Insights

    A check-in calendar, a consistency heatmap and a streak leaderboard, so you can see which habit is slipping.

  • Reminders that adapt

    Up to six a day, repeats between two times, and follow-ups after a missed one. They stop once you're done.

  • iCloud sync

    Off until you switch it on. Then your habits mirror through your own private iCloud database.

  • Unlimited habits

    Go past three active habits and sort them into categories of your own.

  • Daily goals

    Up to 20 check-ins a day, like glasses of water or stretch breaks. The card fills as you tap.

  • Per-day notes

    Tap a day and write down why it went well, or why it didn't.

  • Pause habits

    Away for a week? Pause a habit and the streak waits for you.

A few
good questions.

What can I track?
Simple check-ins, water, reading, time, habits you want to quit, and habits you want to limit. Apple Health tracking is part of Pro. Read the guides.
Do rest days break my streak?
No. Streaks follow each habit's schedule. A day you weren't meant to do the habit doesn't count against you.
Which iPhone do I need?
An iPhone running iOS 26 or later.
Can I use it offline?
Yes. Track habits and browse your history offline. Purchases and optional iCloud sync need an internet connection.
Can I take my data with me?
Yes. Export a JSON backup, restore it later, or export CSV for a spreadsheet. These are available on Free, too.
Who do I contact for help?
Me, directly: support@habitglass.info. Or start with the support page.
