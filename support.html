Email support Common questions

Common questions.

I paid for Pro and it's still locked
Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases. That re-checks your App Store receipt, which is normally all it takes. If the purchase is still waiting on Apple's approval (Ask to Buy, for instance) the app unlocks by itself once that clears. Still stuck after both? Email me and mention which plan you bought.
My streak reset and I don't think it should have
Streaks are counted against the habit's own schedule, so check the Repeat setting first. A three-times-a-week habit and an every-day habit will disagree about the same week. If the schedule looks right and the number still looks wrong, send me the schedule and the days you logged.
Reminders aren't arriving
Reminders are local notifications, so iOS has the final say. Check Settings > Notifications > HabitGlass on your phone, and make sure a Focus mode or Scheduled Summary isn't holding them back. Reminders are set per habit, so confirm that habit has one.
Moving to a new iPhone
An encrypted iPhone backup or an iCloud device transfer brings everything across. By hand, export a JSON backup from Settings on the old phone and import it on the new one. Turning on iCloud sync on both devices also works, and keeps working afterwards.
I want everything deleted
Settings > Data > Delete All Data wipes every habit and check-in from the device, and from your iCloud storage if sync is on. There's nothing on a server of mine to delete, because there is no server. The privacy policy spells out where data lives.
Where's the Android version?
There isn't one, and there's no plan for one. HabitGlass is built directly on iOS 26's Liquid Glass, SwiftData and WidgetKit. A port would be a different app wearing the same name.

How water and reading goals count.

Both split your daily goal into one tick per reminder. The last tick only fills when you reach the goal.

Water

Set a daily goal and a reminder schedule, and HabitGlass splits the goal into one sip per reminder. Say you want 2,000 mL a day with a reminder every hour from 7:30 am to 7:30 pm. That's 13 reminders, so each tick is about 160 mL.

2,000 mL÷13 reminders ≈160 mL a tick

Logging a sip

Log Water opens with 160 mL ready, one reminder's share. Tap Add 160 mL and the card goes 1/13, 2/13 and so on, or nudge the amount first in 50 mL steps. Finish a 500 mL bottle and it jumps 3 ticks at once. The last tick only fills when you actually reach 2,000 mL. The badge beside the bottle turns green and reads Goal Met, the day is done, and the reminders stop. You can still log more after that.

Logged too much?

Tap Adjust, or the amount inside the bottle, to remove 100, 250 or 500 mL, remove a custom amount, or reset today. Resetting asks first and keeps your notes and earlier days. To change the goal itself, edit the habit.

From a reminder or the widget

Tap Complete on a reminder or + on the widget to log a sip without opening the app. HabitGlass adds 160 mL, one reminder's share of your 2,000 mL goal, or just what's left if you're closer than that. Once the goal is met, those taps don't add anything.

Reading

Reading uses the same idea with pages or minutes. Set 30 pages a day with 6 reminders and each tick is 5 pages. Read 12 pages and you're on 2/6. The day completes at 30.

30 pages÷6 reminders =5 pages a tick

Reading by the clock

Set a minutes goal and log the time you read. Pages are optional, so you don't have to move your bookmark. If you finish a book halfway through the day, its pages still count after you start the next one.

From a reminder or the widget

A reading reminder or widget tap opens the reading log, so you can say exactly how much you read. Nothing gets checked off for you.

Still stuck? Write to me.

  • Something's wrong

    Bugs, crashes, a habit that won't behave, a purchase that didn't unlock.

    support@habitglass.info

  • An idea, or a complaint

    Feature requests and design gripes. Both get read, and the good ones get built.

    feedback@habitglass.info

  • Refunds and billing

    Apple handles every payment, so refunds go through them. I can't issue one from here.

    reportaproblem.apple.com

For a faster answer, include

  • Your iPhone model and iOS version (Settings > General > About)
  • The HabitGlass version, at the bottom of the app's Settings
  • What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead
  • A screenshot or screen recording, if it's visual

Don't send an export of your habit data unless I ask for it. I don't need it to fix most things.
