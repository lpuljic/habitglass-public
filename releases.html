v1.4.0 October 2026

More control, clearer reminders

A more personal HabitGlass.

A guided start, a more personal Home, and a new Today widget.

✨New Features

  • Try the interactive welcome tour: check in on a sample habit, choose a Home layout, and preview a reminder.
  • Compare every Pro plan at the end of the tour, or upgrade from Home when you reach the three-habit free limit. Not Now keeps you on Free.
  • Choose which day starts your week in Settings › General. Calendars, weekly goals, and streaks follow your choice; past weekly results are recalculated.
  • Choose a custom background, or let its colors shift with the time of day.
  • Preview and manage your habit reminders together in Settings › Notifications.
  • Add the Today widget to see the day's habits on your Home Screen and check them off as you go. Touch and hold it to choose which habits it lists.

💎Improvements

  • Settings opens in a large sheet, with dedicated pages for General, Appearance, Notifications, About, Support, and Data & Backup.
  • The Habit Focus widget shows how many habits remain today and follows Home's order when choosing the next one.
  • Habit cards have clearer, softer glass surfaces. Preview app icons and Glass Plant icon backgrounds before setting them.

🐞Fixes

  • Reminders now respect each habit's scheduled days and start date, refresh after iCloud changes, clear when habits are turned off or archived, and open the right habit or logging screen.
  • Widget ticks and icons stay visible on Clear and Tinted Home Screens. Habit Focus keeps the same next habit as Home after backup imports and iCloud sync.

v1.2.0 Released October 2026

The Home update

A calmer start. A clearer Home.

A new welcome, a Home that keeps today apart from this week, and Insights that show how you're doing.

✨New Features

  • A new welcome: a trail of light climbs toward sunrise as you set up your first habits and try your first check-in.
  • Redesigned Insights show your completion, the trend against the previous period, and a Coach's Take, from 7 days up to a year. Tap any day in the calendar to see what you finished.
  • Home now sorts weekly goals into This week, habits that aren't due into Not today, and finished habits into Done, so a 3-times-a-week habit no longer counts against today. Turn on Collapse Done in Settings › Home Layout to fold finished habits away until tomorrow.
  • Habit cards lead with today's progress, your current streak, and your month as daily bars, in every Home layout and in widgets. Water and reading goals show on every card.
  • Choose your theme, app color, and app icon under Appearance. Keep the original icon or pick a new Light or Dark plant icon.
  • After you add a habit, HabitGlass offers to set a daily reminder for it.
  • Fix a reading log from the Adjust menu: undo the last log, remove pages or minutes, or reset today. Your bookmark moves back with removed pages.
  • Feel a tap when opening or leaving habit details, or editing habits. Turn off haptics in Settings.

💎Improvements

  • Habit cards and widgets count your month in days, like 22/27 days, and weekly goals count the weeks you hit, like 3/4 weeks.
  • Bubble rows and Mini constellations are now called Rows and Tiles.
  • Insights, habit details, and widgets feel quicker, especially with a long history.

🐞Fixes

  • Reminders you set up before allowing notifications now start as soon as you allow them, without reopening the app.

Home

v1.1.0 Released September 2026

Free for everyone

A clearer way to keep going.

Make Home yours. Log every drink and every page.

🎨UI Changes

  • Choose Cards, Bubble rows, or Mini constellations for Home.
  • Preview layouts in Settings, then switch with dots or a swipe.
  • Choose an app accent color with presets or a custom color picker, then keep or tint the original background separately.
  • Keep Home screen icons neutral or have them follow the selected accent.
  • Home layout dots follow the selected app accent.

✨New Features

  • Personalize habits and custom categories with full-color emoji or SF Symbols.
  • Attach books to Read a Book, log pages, and keep finished books in history.
  • See your book in 3D while logging, with a bookmark that moves to the next page.
  • Track water against a daily goal with a live bottle and quick amount controls.
  • Reach your water goal and the bottle celebrates with a shimmer and a Goal Met check.

v1.0.1 Released September 2026

The polish update

Your habits, easier to shape.

Put routines in your order, understand progress faster, and spend less time hunting for useful actions.

✨New

  • Insights and habit cards now show clearer month and day labels in consistency views.
  • Reorder habit cards to keep your most important routines within easy reach.
  • Create custom habit colors with the new color picker.
  • Long-press hints make useful habit actions easier to discover.

💎Improvements

  • Completed habits can move to the bottom of the list automatically.
  • Refined the look and feel of Settings.
  • Improved layout consistency across Category, Reminders, Repeat, and related sheets.
  • Notifications now show progress, such as “1 of 3,” for habits with multiple daily completions.

🐞Fixes

  • Calendar edits now update habit history and statistics immediately.
  • Skip Intro now dismisses onboarding correctly.
  • Selected controls remain readable with light and custom habit colors.
  • Core habit, category, statistics, and consistency layouts now adapt to accessibility text sizes.
  • Calendars and weekday selectors now follow the device’s regional week order.
  • Category sheets now expand correctly without repeated layout warnings.
  • Fixed cases where reminder and habit-card state could become stuck.
