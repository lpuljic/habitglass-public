v1.0.1 Released September 2026

The polish update

Your habits, easier to shape.

Put routines in your order, understand progress faster, and spend less time hunting for useful actions.

✨New

  • See clearer month and day labels in consistency views.
  • Reorder habit cards to keep important routines close.
  • Create custom habit colors with the new color picker.
  • Long-press hints reveal useful habit actions.

💎Improvements

  • Move completed habits to the bottom automatically.
  • Enjoy a more polished Settings experience.
  • See more consistent layouts across Category, Reminders, Repeat, and related sheets.
  • Track multi-completion goals in notifications with progress such as “1 of 3.”

🐞Fixes

  • Calendar edits update history and stats immediately.
  • Skip Intro now dismisses onboarding correctly.
  • Selected controls remain readable with light and custom habit colors.
  • Prevented a crash when SwiftUI renders accent colors on Home and Settings.
  • Core habit, category, statistics, and consistency layouts now adapt to accessibility text sizes.
  • Calendars and weekday selectors now follow the device's regional week order.
  • Category sheets now expand correctly without repeated layout warnings.
  • Reminder and habit-card state no longer gets stuck.
